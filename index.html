Getting Started

Sit with three songs each morning and three each evening, about four minutes apiece, all from one album. Each album moves from navel songs through heart songs to third eye songs, so the first song is for the navel chakra, the second for the heart, the third for the third eye.

With time for only one song, meditate on the heart. If you need more grounding, take the navel instead.

Six songs: two for the navel, then two for the heart, then two for the third eye.

Twelve songs: four for each, in the same order.

Choose an album above the song counts, or let each sit bring a new one. Mix It Up draws the songs from across the albums instead.

The yantra draws itself once with each song. Tap the screen to show End sitting.

Keep it on your phone: on Android, open the menu and choose Install app or Add to Home screen. On iPhone, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.

Your sits are remembered on this device, under Your practice. They are not sent anywhere. Morning and evening reminders, if you want them, are set on that same page. Settings holds a name for this device, Spotify, and sign-out.

About the Music

The music is by Vayu Meditation. The sangha's recordings play right here; albums not yet recorded play from your own Spotify account (Premium).

A sit keeps to one album. Mix It Up follows the morning and evening sets of the Heart School meditation app, made for the lineage's students.
