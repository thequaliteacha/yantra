Begin your morning meditation

Each song runs three to four minutes, so three songs make about twelve minutes and six songs about twenty-four.

Getting Started

Sit with three songs each morning and three each evening, about four minutes apiece. The first song is for the navel chakra, the second for the heart, the third for the third eye.

With time for only one song, meditate on the heart. If you need more grounding, take the navel instead.

Six songs: two for the navel, then two for the heart, then two for the third eye.

Twelve songs: four for each, in the same order.

The yantra draws itself once with each song. Tap the screen to show End sitting.

Keep it on your phone: on Android, open the menu and choose Install app or Add to Home screen. On iPhone, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.

About the Music

The music is by Vayu Meditation and plays from your own Spotify account (Premium). Nothing is copied or stored here.

The morning and evening sets follow the Heart School meditation app, made for the lineage's students.
